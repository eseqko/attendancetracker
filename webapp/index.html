Loading the Attendance Tracker…

The first visit downloads the app (about 85 MB) and can take a minute or two on school Wi‑Fi; after that it starts in seconds.

This page is public like any website, but it contains only the app — no student data. Files you upload are opened and analyzed entirely inside your browser on this computer; nothing is ever sent to any server, and the site cannot see your data. Closing or reloading the tab erases everything, so keep your caseload and report files handy.
